# 4-2. Experiment with layer controls

The **Layer controls** section of a layer card toggles the interactive
controls the Explorer shows for that layer.

1. Edit your *AGB* layer card and scroll down to the **Layer controls**
   section.
2. Toggle a few of the controls on or off:
   - **Zoom to**
   - **Opacity slider**
   - **Blend mode**
   - **Download**
   - **Constraint slider**

    Leave the **Temporal controls** alone for now — we'll come back to those
    in the Time Series tutorial.
3. **Save** the layer card and open **GE Preview** to see the controls appear
   in the Explorer.

!!! tip "Download links"
    For **Download**, you can either paste the COG URL from the previous
    tutorial (which will trigger a direct download) or a link to somewhere
    more useful — for example the dataset's STAC record on the PRR.
